Our Story

The Heritage of Uru Minor Seminary

For generations, our doors have been open to young men seeking a faith-filled education in a distinguished Catholic environment.

History

A Tradition of Faith and Scholarship

Founded to nurture spiritual growth and academic rigor, Uru Minor Seminary has been a place of formation, prayer, and leadership for students across Tanzania. Our commitment is to uphold Catholic values while preparing students for the modern world.

Why Uru Minor Seminary?

  • Strong academic programs with pastoral care
  • Well-developed spiritual formation curriculum
  • Respect for tradition and intellectual growth
